A comparative study of optimization algorithms in physics-informed neural networks for singularly perturbed differential equations

Nishanth J, Elango S. Applied Soft Computing, 2026

I trained physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) to solve singularly perturbed differential equations, and compared five gradient-based optimizers to find the most stable and accurate setup. The models reached 95% accuracy against known analytical solutions.

Read the paper (DOI)

What I did

  • Designed PINN models for singularly perturbed differential equations, where standard numerical methods struggle.
  • Compared Adagrad, Momentum, Adadelta, Nesterov Accelerated Gradient and RMSprop to find the most stable and accurate training setup.
  • Used linear algebra, differential equations and feed-forward neural network theory to connect classical numerical methods with modern deep learning.
  • Implemented the work in Python with TensorFlow and wrote it up in LaTeX (Overleaf).
