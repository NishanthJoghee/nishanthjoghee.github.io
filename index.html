About me

I am an AI/ML Engineer at Integrin Enterprise Solutions in Coimbatore, where I am building an unsupervised system to detect suspicious pharmaceutical orders and cut manual fraud review.

I hold an M.Sc. in Mathematics from Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, where I graduated as a gold medalist and ranked first in the program. My master's work on neural networks for differential equations became a paper in Applied Soft Computing.

I work mostly in Python, with TensorFlow, Scikit-learn, LangChain, FAISS and Hugging Face.

Peer-reviewed research

A comparative study of optimization algorithms in physics-informed neural networks for singularly perturbed differential equations

Nishanth J, Elango S. Applied Soft Computing, 2026

I trained physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) to solve singularly perturbed differential equations, and compared five gradient-based optimizers to find the most stable and accurate setup. The models reached 95% accuracy against known analytical solutions.
